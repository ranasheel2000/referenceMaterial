# Grammar

## 1. ～らしい (rashii)

### Meaning

It seems...
Apparently...
I think ... based on objective evidence.

### Key Points

- Used when making a judgment based on **objective information**.
- The speaker has some evidence such as something they heard, read, or observed.
- Less subjective than **ようだ**.
- Common in news reports and everyday conversations.

### Pattern

- Noun + らしい
- Verb (plain form) + らしい
- い-adjective + らしい
- な-adjective + らしい

### Examples

- 火事かじの原因げんいんは、たばこの火ひの消けし忘わすれらしい。
  - *Apparently the fire was caused by a cigarette that wasn't put out.*

- 彼かれは最近さいきん忙いそがしいらしい。
  - *Apparently he has been busy recently.*

- いつもトマトだけ残のこすから、彼かれはトマトが嫌きらいらしい。
  - *Since he always leaves tomatoes, it seems he doesn't like them.*

- コンサートが終おわったらしく、大勢おおぜいの人ひとが出でてきた。
  - *The concert seemed to have ended because many people came outside.*

### Difference from ようだ

- **らしい** → objective evidence
- **ようだ** → speaker's own impression

Example:

彼女は結婚したらしい。
→ I heard she got married.

彼女は幸せなようだ。
→ She looks happy (that's my impression).

---

## 2. ～そうだ／～そうにもない (Appearance & Prediction)

### Meaning

Looks like...
Seems likely to...
Looks unlikely to...

### Key Points

- Expresses a prediction based on what you see right now.
- Often describes something that is about to happen.
- Frequently used with:
  - Potential forms
  - Intransitive verbs
  - Non-volitional verbs

### Pattern

**Affirmative**

- Verb stem + そうだ

**Negative**

- Verb stem + そうにない
- Verb stem + そうもない
- Verb stem + そうにもない

### Examples

- 初心者しょしんしゃですが、この曲きょくなら弾ひけそうです。
  - *Even though I'm a beginner, I think I can play this song.*

- 雨あめが降ふりそうです。
  - *It looks like it's going to rain.*

- 今日は忙いそがしくて終おわりそうにありません。
  - *It doesn't look like I'll finish today.*

- 心配しんぱいで今夜こんやは眠ねむれそうもありません。
  - *I'm so worried that I don't think I'll be able to sleep.*

### Common Mistake

❌ 雨が降るそうです。

This means:

"I heard it will rain."

(Not appearance.)

✔ 雨が降りそうです。

"It looks like it's going to rain."

## 3. ～ようだ (you da)

### Meaning

It seems...
It appears...
It looks like...

### Key Points

- Used when making an inference based on:
  - what you see
  - what you hear
  - what you feel
  - your own judgment
- More subjective than **らしい**.
- Frequently translated as "it seems."

### Pattern

- Noun + のようだ
- Verb (plain) + ようだ
- い-adjective + ようだ
- な-adjective + なようだ

### Examples

- ノックしても返事へんじがないので、留守るすのようです。
  - *There is no answer, so it seems nobody is home.*

- あの店みせは人気にんきがあるようです。
  - *That restaurant seems popular.*

- 彼かれは疲つかれているようだ。
  - *He looks tired.*

- この料理りょうりはあまりおいしくないようです。
  - *This food doesn't seem very delicious.*

### Difference from らしい

らしい
→ Based on objective information.

ようだ
→ Based on your own observation or impression.

Example:

ニュースによると、台風たいふうが来くるらしい。
→ According to the news, a typhoon is coming.

空そらが暗くらい。台風たいふうが来くるようだ。
→ The sky is dark. It looks like a typhoon is coming.

## 4. ～ば／～たら／～といい

### Meaning

You should...
It would be good if...
Why don't you...

Used to give advice or recommendations.

### Key Points

There are slight differences in nuance.

- ～ばいい
  - Direct.
  - Can sound a little blunt.

- ～たらいい
  - Softer.
  - Most common in conversation.

- ～といい
  - Gentle advice.
  - Common between friends and family.

### Pattern

- Verbば + いい
- Verbたら + いい
- Dictionary form + といい

### Examples

- 寝ねられない時ときは、温あたたかい飲のみ物ものを飲のむといいですよ。
  - *If you can't sleep, you should drink something warm.*

- 京都きょうとのことなら山田やまださんに聞きいたらいいですよ。
  - *If it's about Kyoto, you should ask Yamada.*

- そんなに大変たいへんな仕事しごとなら、やめればいい。
  - *If it's that difficult, you should just quit.*

- わからなかったら先生せんせいに聞きけばいいですよ。
  - *If you don't understand, ask the teacher.*

### Nuance

ばいい
→ More direct

たらいい
→ Softer

といい
→ Sounds like friendly advice



## 5. ～ば～のに (ba... noni)

### Meaning

If only...
If ... had happened...
Things would have been different if...

### Key Points

- Expresses **regret**, **disappointment**, or **frustration**.
- Refers to a situation that **did not happen**.
- Often implies that the outcome would have been better if the condition had been met.
- Frequently translated as **"if only..."**.

### Pattern

- Verb (ば-form) + のに
- い-adjective + ければ + のに
- な-adjective + なら（ば）+ のに
- Noun + なら（ば）+ のに

### Examples

- あと５分早はやく家いえを出でれば、間まに合あったのに。
  - *If only I had left home five minutes earlier, I would have made it.*

- もう少すこし安やすければ、買かえるのに。
  - *If it were a little cheaper, I could buy it.*

- 彼かれがもっと真面目まじめなら、別わかれなかったのに。
  - *If only he had been more sincere, we wouldn't have broken up.*

- 毎日勉強べんきょうすれば、試験しけんで困こまらなかったのに。
  - *If only I had studied every day, I wouldn't have struggled on the exam.*

### Common Usage

This grammar is often used when talking to yourself or expressing regret about the past.

Example:

もっと早はやく寝ねればよかったのに。
→ *If only I had gone to bed earlier.*

---

## 6. ～さえ～ば (sae... ba)

### Meaning

As long as...
If only...
Provided that...

### Key Points

- Expresses that **one condition is enough**.
- Everything else is considered unimportant.
- Often translated as **"as long as..."**.

### Pattern

- Noun + でさえあれば
- Verb (ます-stem) + さえすれば
- い-adjective + ければ
- な-adjective + でさえあれば

### Examples

- この仕事しごとさえ終おわれば、帰かえれます。
  - *As long as I finish this work, I can go home.*

- 子こどもが健康けんこうでさえあれば、親おやは安心あんしんです。
  - *As long as the children are healthy, parents are happy.*

- 安やすくさえあれば、どんなパソコンでもいいです。
  - *Any computer is fine as long as it's cheap.*

- レシピを読よみさえすれば、誰だれでも作つくれます。
  - *Anyone can make it as long as they read the recipe.*

### Difference from ～ば

普通の条件：
お金があれば買います。
→ *If I have money, I'll buy it.*

強調：
お金さえあれば買います。
→ *As long as I have money, I'll buy it.*

The second sentence emphasizes that **money is the only thing that matters**.

---

## 7. ～ても (temo)

### Meaning

Even if...
Even though...

### Key Points

- Expresses a result that is **contrary to expectation**.
- The second clause happens regardless of the first.
- Very common in both spoken and written Japanese.

### Pattern

- Verb (て-form) + も
- い-adjective + くても
- な-adjective + でも
- Noun + でも

### Examples

- 雨あめが降ふっても、試合しあいはあります。
  - *Even if it rains, the game will be held.*

- 暑あつくても、エアコンをつけません。
  - *Even if it's hot, I don't use the air conditioner.*

- 大変たいへんでも、この仕事しごとはやりたいです。
  - *Even if it's difficult, I want to do this job.*

- 熱ねつがあっても学校がっこうへ行いきます。
  - *Even if I have a fever, I'll go to school.*

- 今いま分わからなくても心配しんぱいしなくていいです。
  - *Even if you don't understand now, you don't need to worry.*

### Casual Conversation

In casual speech:

- ～ても → ～たって

Example:

雨あめが降ふったって行いくよ。
→ *I'll go even if it rains.*

---

## 8. ～てもかまわない (temo kamawanai)

### Meaning

It's okay if...
I don't mind if...
You may...

### Key Points

- Expresses **permission** or **acceptance**.
- Similar to **～てもいい**, but sounds slightly more formal.
- Also used to ask permission.

### Pattern

- Verb (て-form) + もかまわない
- Verb (て-form) + もかまいません

Question:

- ～てもかまいませんか。

### Examples

- 明日あしたでもかまいません。
  - *Tomorrow is fine.*

- 家賃やちんが安やすければ、駅えきから遠とおくてもかまいません。
  - *I don't mind if it's far from the station as long as the rent is cheap.*

- 車くるまがあるので、不便ふべんでもかまいません。
  - *I have a car, so I don't mind if it's inconvenient.*

- ここで写真しゃしんを撮とってもかまいませんか。
  - *May I take pictures here?*

- 少すこし遅おくれてもかまいません。
  - *It's okay if you're a little late.*

# Commonly Confused

### らしい vs ようだ vs そうだ

**～らしい**
- Based on information you've heard or objective evidence.

> 明日雨らしい。
> *Apparently it will rain tomorrow.*

**～ようだ**
- Based on your own observation or impression.

> 空が暗い。雨のようだ。
> *The sky is dark. It seems like rain.*

**～そうだ（appearance）**
- Something looks like it's about to happen.

> 雨が降りそうだ。
> *It looks like it's going to rain.*

---

### ～ば～のに vs ～さえ～ば

**～ば～のに**
- Expresses regret.

> もっと勉強すればよかったのに。
> *If only I had studied more.*

**～さえ～ば**
- Expresses the minimum requirement.

> 時間さえあれば行けます。
> *As long as I have time, I can go.*

---

# Study Tips

1. **らしい・ようだ・そうだ** are all translated as **"seems"**, but they are used differently:
   - **らしい** → objective information
   - **ようだ** → your own impression
   - **そうだ** → appearance or immediate prediction

2. **～ば～のに** almost always expresses **regret**.

3. **～さえ～ば** means **"that's all I need."**

4. **～ても** expresses **unexpected results**.

5. **～てもかまわない** is a polite way to express **permission** or **acceptance**, and is slightly more formal than **～てもいい**.

# Additional Vocabulary Items

## Vocabulary Details

### うきうき (uki uki)

**Meaning:** Excited; cheerful; looking forward to something

### Key Points

- Describes a happy, light-hearted feeling.
- Often used when anticipating something enjoyable.
- Usually appears as **うきうきする**.

### Examples

- もうすぐ旅行りょこうなので、毎日まいにちうきうきしています。
  - *I'm excited because my trip is coming up soon.*

- 子どもたちはプレゼントをもらって、うきうきしていた。
  - *The children were excited after receiving their presents.*

- 春はるになると、なんだかうきうきした気分きぶんになる。
  - *When spring comes, I somehow feel cheerful.*

---

## どきどき (doki doki)

**Meaning:** Heart pounding; nervous; excited

### Key Points

- Describes a fast heartbeat caused by:
  - nervousness
  - excitement
  - fear
  - anticipation

- Usually used as **どきどきする**.

### Examples

- 面接めんせつの前まえでどきどきしています。
  - *I'm nervous before my interview.*

- 好すきな人ひとに話はなしかけられて、どきどきした。
  - *My heart started pounding when the person I like talked to me.*

- ジェットコースターに乗のる前まえはいつもどきどきする。
  - *I always get nervous before riding a roller coaster.*

---

## いらいら (ira ira)

**Meaning:** Irritated; frustrated; annoyed

### Key Points

- Used when things don't go as expected.
- Often appears as **いらいらする**.

### Examples

- バスがなかなか来こなくていらいらした。
  - *I got frustrated because the bus wouldn't come.*

- 最近さいきん仕事しごとが忙いそがしくて、いらいらしている。
  - *I've been feeling irritated because work has been busy lately.*

- 待またされるといらいらしてしまう。
  - *I get irritated when people keep me waiting.*

---

## がっかり (gakkari)

**Meaning:** Disappointed

### Key Points

- Used when reality does not match expectations.
- Often used with **～する**.

### Examples

- 試験しけんに落おちてがっかりした。
  - *I was disappointed because I failed the exam.*

- 楽たのしみにしていた映画えいががつまらなくてがっかりした。
  - *I was disappointed because the movie wasn't interesting.*

- 売うり切きれていてがっかりした。
  - *I was disappointed because it was sold out.*

---

## ほっと (hotto)

**Meaning:** Relieved; feel at ease

### Key Points

- Used after worry or stress disappears.
- Common expression:
  - **ほっとする**

### Examples

- テストが終おわってほっとした。
  - *I felt relieved after the exam finished.*

- 無事ぶじに帰かえってきてほっとしました。
  - *I was relieved that they returned safely.*

- 財布さいふが見みつかってほっとした。
  - *I was relieved that I found my wallet.*

---

## かっと (katto)

**Meaning:** Fly into a rage; lose one's temper

### Key Points

- Describes sudden anger.
- Usually followed by **する**.

### Examples

- かっとして大おおきな声こえを出だしてしまった。
  - *I lost my temper and shouted.*

- 悪口わるぐちを言いわれて、かっとした。
  - *I got angry after being insulted.*

- かっとなって物ものを投なげてしまった。
  - *I became furious and threw something.*

---

## むっと (mutto)

**Meaning:** Feel offended; silently annoyed

### Key Points

- Weaker than **かっとする**.
- The anger is usually kept inside.
- Often caused by someone's attitude or words.

### Examples

- 失礼しつれいな言いい方かたにむっとした。
  - *I felt offended by the rude way they spoke.*

- 店員てんいんの態度たいどにむっとした。
  - *I was annoyed by the clerk's attitude.*

- その一言ひとことで彼女かのじょはむっとしたようだ。
  - *She seemed offended by that remark.*

---

## くよくよ (kuyo kuyo)

**Meaning:** Worry excessively; dwell on something

### Key Points

- Used when someone keeps worrying about something that has already happened.
- Usually appears as **くよくよする**.

### Examples

- 終おわったことをくよくよしても仕方しかたがない。
  - *There's no point worrying about something that's already over.*

- 彼かれは小ちいさな失敗しっぱいでもくよくよする。
  - *He worries even about small mistakes.*

- あまりくよくよしないほうがいいよ。
  - *You shouldn't dwell on it too much.*

---

## はらはら (hara hara)

**Meaning:** Feel anxious while watching

### Key Points

- Used when watching a dangerous or uncertain situation.
- Usually followed by **する** or **させる**.

### Examples

- 子こどもの運転うんてんを見みていてはらはらした。
  - *Watching my child drive made me nervous.*

- 綱渡つなわたりを見みて、はらはらした。
  - *Watching the tightrope walker made me anxious.*

- 映画えいがの最後さいごまではらはらしっぱなしだった。
  - *The movie kept me on the edge of my seat until the end.*

---

## ぞっと (zotto)

**Meaning:** Be horrified; shiver with fear

### Key Points

- Expresses sudden fear or horror.
- Can also describe shivering because of cold.

### Examples

- 怖こわい話はなしを聞きいてぞっとした。
  - *I shivered after hearing the scary story.*

- 鏡かがみに知しらない人ひとの顔かおが映うつっていてぞっとした。
  - *I was horrified to see a stranger's face in the mirror.*

- その事故じこを想像そうぞうしただけでぞっとする。
  - *Just imagining the accident makes me shiver.*

# Study Tips

Many Japanese mimetic words (オノマトペ) describe emotions. Rather than translating them literally, try to associate them with situations:

- **うきうき** → Before a holiday or birthday.
- **どきどき** → Before an exam or confession.
- **いらいら** → Waiting in traffic.
- **がっかり** → Receiving bad news.
- **ほっと** → After finishing a difficult task.
- **かっと** → Losing your temper.
- **むっと** → Feeling quietly offended.
- **くよくよ** → Regretting the past.
- **はらはら** → Watching someone do something dangerous.
- **ぞっと** → Hearing or seeing something frightening.

# Commonly Confused Kanji

### 表情 vs 様子 vs 姿

**表情（ひょうじょう）**
- Facial expression only.

> 彼女は悲しい表情をしている。
> *She has a sad expression.*

---

**様子（ようす）**
- Situation, condition, or how something appears.

> 外の様子を見てきます。
> *I'll go check what's happening outside.*

---

**姿（すがた）**
- Physical appearance, figure, or shape.

> 子どもの姿が見えない。
> *I can't see the child.*

---

### 謝る vs 許す

**謝る**
- To apologize.

> 友達に謝った。
> *I apologized to my friend.*

**許す**
- To forgive.

> 友達が許してくれた。
> *My friend forgave me.*

Remember:

- **謝る** → The person who made the mistake.
- **許す** → The person who forgives.

# Study Tips

### 1. Learn the Three "Seems"

Many learners confuse **らしい**, **ようだ**, and **そうだ**.

A simple way to remember them:

- **らしい** → *I heard so.* (objective information)
- **ようだ** → *I think so.* (my own observation)
- **そうだ** → *It looks like it's about to happen.* (appearance)

### 2. Remember the Conditional Patterns

- **～ば～のに**
  - Regret
  - *If only...*

- **～さえ～ば**
  - One thing is enough.
  - *As long as...*

- **～ても**
  - The result doesn't change.
  - *Even if...*
