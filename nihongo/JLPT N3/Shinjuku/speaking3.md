# Japanese Auxiliary & Functional Grammar Cheat Sheet

These patterns express intention, completion, change, expectation, regret, probability, obligation, and other meanings that English often expresses with auxiliary verbs or special verb phrases.

---

# About To Do

## Pattern

```
〜ところだ
```

### Examples

今行くところです。

> I'm about to leave.

今食べるところです。

> I'm about to eat.

---
