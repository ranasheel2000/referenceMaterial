
# Japanese Tense System for Speaking

Japanese grammar has only **two true grammatical tenses**:

1. Non-past (Present + Future)
2. Past

Unlike English, Japanese does not have separate grammatical tenses for:

- Present Continuous
- Present Perfect
- Past Perfect
- Future Continuous

Instead Japanese uses:

- verb forms
- helper expressions
- particles
- time expressions
- context

---

# 1. Non-Past (Present / Future)

Japanese uses the same form for present and future.

## Pattern

### Polite


Verb ます


### Casual


Verb dictionary form


---

## Meaning

Depending on context:

- I do
- I usually do
- I will do

---

## Examples

### Present


毎日日本語を勉強します。

I study Japanese every day.


### Future


明日日本語を勉強します。

I will study Japanese tomorrow.


---

# 2. Past

Used for completed actions.

## Pattern

### Polite


Verb ました


### Casual


Verb past form


---

## Examples


昨日映画を見ました。

I watched a movie yesterday.

昨日映画を見た。

I watched a movie yesterday.


---

# 3. Negative Present

Equivalent:

- do not
- don't
- will not

---

## Pattern

### Polite


Verb ません


### Casual


Verb ない


---

## Examples


肉を食べません。

I don't eat meat.

肉を食べない。

I don't eat meat.


---

# 4. Negative Past

Equivalent:

- did not do

---

## Pattern

### Polite


Verb ませんでした


### Casual


Verb なかった


---

## Examples


昨日食べませんでした。

I did not eat yesterday.

昨日食べなかった。

I didn't eat yesterday.


---

# 5. Progressive 〜ています

Equivalent:

- am doing
- is doing
- are doing

---

## Pattern


Verb て-form + います


---

# Current Action

Example:


今食べています。

I am eating now.


---

# Continuous State

Japanese also uses ています for permanent/current states.

---

## Living


東京に住んでいます。

I live in Tokyo.


---

## Working


会社で働いています。

I work at a company.


---

## Knowing


知っています。

I know.


---

# 6. Past Progressive 〜ていました

Equivalent:

- was doing
- were doing

---

## Pattern


Verb て-form + いました


---

## Examples


昨日勉強していました。

I was studying yesterday.

その時テレビを見ていました。

I was watching TV at that time.


---

# 7. Habitual Actions

Japanese uses the non-past tense.

---

## Pattern


Frequency word + Verb


---

## Common Frequency Words


いつも
always

よく
often

時々
sometimes

毎日
every day

毎週
every week


---

## Examples


毎日コーヒーを飲みます。

I drink coffee every day.

週末によく映画を見ます。

I often watch movies on weekends.


---

# 8. Present Perfect Equivalent

English:

"I have already done."

Japanese uses:


もう + Past tense


---

## Examples


もう食べました。

I already ate.

もう終わりました。

I already finished.


---

# Not Yet

English:

"I haven't done yet."

## Pattern


まだ + Verb て-form + いません


---

## Examples


まだ食べていません。

I haven't eaten yet.

まだ終わっていません。

It is not finished yet.


---

# 9. Experience 〜たことがある

English:

"I have done before."

---

## Pattern


Verb past + ことがあります


---

## Examples


日本へ行ったことがあります。

I have been to Japan.

寿司を食べたことがあります。

I have eaten sushi.


---

# 10. Never Experience

English:

"I have never done."

---

## Pattern 1


一度も + Verb negative


---

## Pattern 2


Verb past + ことがありません


---

## Examples


一度も日本へ行ったことがありません。

I have never been to Japan.

寿司を食べたことがありません。

I have never eaten sushi.


---

# 11. Duration (For / Since)

## For

English:

"I have studied Japanese for two years."

---

## Pattern


Duration + ています


---

## Examples


日本語を2年間勉強しています。

I have studied Japanese for two years.

東京に5年間住んでいます。

I have lived in Tokyo for five years.


---

# Since

## Pattern


Time + から + ています


---

## Examples


朝から働いています。

I have been working since morning.

9時から勉強しています。

I have been studying since 9.


---

# 12. Just Did

English:

"I just did."

---

## Pattern


たった今 + Past


---

## Examples


たった今帰りました。

I just returned.

たった今食べました。

I just ate.


---

## A Little While Ago


さっき + Past


Example:


さっき食べました。

I ate a little while ago.


---

# 13. About To Do

English:

"I am about to do."

---

## Pattern


もうすぐ + Verb


---

## Examples


もうすぐ始まります。

It will start soon.

もうすぐ出ます。

I am leaving soon.


---

# 14. Before / After

## Before

Pattern:


Verb dictionary form + 前に


---

Examples:


寝る前に本を読みます。

I read before sleeping.

食べる前に手を洗います。

I wash hands before eating.


---

## After

Pattern:


Verb past + 後で


---

Examples:


食べた後で勉強します。

I study after eating.

仕事の後で会います。

I meet after work.


---

# 15. Past Perfect Equivalent

English:

"I had eaten before he arrived."

Japanese does not have a special past perfect tense.

Use:

- 前に (before)
- 後で (after)
- chronological order

---

## Examples


彼が来る前に食べました。

I ate before he came.

映画を見た後で寝ました。

I slept after watching a movie.


---

# 16. Completed State 〜てあります

Meaning:

Something has been intentionally done and the result remains.

---

## Pattern


Verb て-form + あります


---

## Examples


予約してあります。

The reservation has been made.

準備してあります。

It has been prepared.


---

## Difference

### しました

Someone performed an action.


予約しました。

I made a reservation.


---

### してあります

The result exists.


予約してあります。

The reservation is already prepared.


---

# 17. Change of State 〜になる

Meaning:

- become
- get
- turn into

---

# Noun

Pattern:


Noun + になる


Example:


先生になりました。

I became a teacher.


---

# na-Adjective

Pattern:


na-adjective + になる


Example:


元気になりました。

I became healthy.


---

# i-Adjective

Pattern:


i-adjective + くなる


Example:


寒くなりました。

It became cold.

日本語が上手になりました。

My Japanese became better.
