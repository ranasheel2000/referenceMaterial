Japanese adjectives are extremely important for speaking because they combine directly with:

nouns (大きい家 = big house)
feelings (嬉しいです = I am happy)
opinions (面白いと思います = I think it is interesting)
comparisons (もっと大きい = bigger)

i-adjectives are called so because their dictionary form usually ends with い.

高い
takai
expensive / high

安い
yasui
cheap

美味しい
oishii
delicious



Present:
高いです
It is expensive

Negative:
高くないです
It is not expensive

Past:
高かったです
It was expensive

Past Negative:
高くなかったです
It was not expensive

# 12. Essential i-Adjective Patterns

## Very

とても + adjective

Example:

とても美味しいです。

It is very delicious.

---

## A little

少し + adjective

Example:

少し高いです。

It is a little expensive.

---

## More

もっと + adjective

Example:

もっと大きいです。

It is bigger.

---

## Too much

Too + adjective:

Too expensive:

高すぎます。

Too expensive.
