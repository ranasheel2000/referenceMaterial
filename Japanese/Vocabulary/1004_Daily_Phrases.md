# Japanese Daily Phrases.md

## Purpose

This file contains high-frequency Japanese phrases used in everyday conversations.

Focus:
- Speaking naturally
- Reacting quickly
- Handling common situations
- Building conversation confidence

---

# 1. Greetings

## Good Morning

### おはようございます
**Ohayou gozaimasu**

Good morning (polite)

Casual:

### おはよう
**Ohayou**

Good morning

---

## Hello

### こんにちは
**Konnichiwa**

Hello / Good afternoon

---

## Good Evening

### こんばんは
**Konbanwa**

Good evening

---

## Good Night

### おやすみなさい
**Oyasumi nasai**

Good night

Casual:

### おやすみ
**Oyasumi**

---

## Goodbye

### さようなら
**Sayounara**

Goodbye

Usually used for long separations.

More natural daily usage:

### またね
**Mata ne**

See you

### また明日
**Mata ashita**

See you tomorrow

---

# 2. Introducing Yourself

## My name is...

### 私は ___ です
**Watashi wa ___ desu**

I am ___.

Example:

私はジョンです。

My name is John.

---

## Nice to Meet You

### はじめまして
**Hajimemashite**

Nice to meet you.

---

## Please Treat Me Well

### よろしくお願いします
**Yoroshiku onegaishimasu**

Nice to meet you / Please take care of me.

Very important Japanese expression.

Used when:
- meeting someone
- starting work
- requesting something

---

## I Am From...

### ___から来ました

I came from ___.

Example:

アメリカから来ました。

I am from America.

---

## I Live In...

### ___に住んでいます

I live in ___.

Example:

東京に住んでいます。

I live in Tokyo.

---

## My Job Is...

### 仕事は___です

My job is ___.

Example:

エンジニアです。

I am an engineer.

---
