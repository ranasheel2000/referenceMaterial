For speaking, adverbs are extremely important because they add time, frequency, certainty, emotion, and natural flow to simple sentences.

Example:

Without adverbs:

日本へ行きます。
I will go to Japan.

With adverbs:

たぶん来年日本へもう一度行きます。
I will probably go to Japan once again next year.



# Japanese Adverbs.md

## Purpose

High-frequency Japanese adverbs used in everyday conversations.

Adverbs usually come before the verb or adjective they modify.

Example:

ゆっくり話す

Speak slowly.

とても美味しい

Very delicious.

---

# 1. Frequency Adverbs

## いつも

**itsumo**

Always / Usually

Example:

いつもコーヒーを飲みます。

I always drink coffee.

---

## よく

**yoku**

Often / Well

Examples:

よく映画を見ます。

I often watch movies.

日本語がよく分かります。

I understand Japanese well.

---

## 時々

**tokidoki**

Sometimes

Example:

時々料理します。

I sometimes cook.

---

## たまに

**tamani**

Occasionally

Example:

たまに外食します。

I occasionally eat out.

---

## あまり

**amari**

Not much (usually with negative)

Example:

あまり食べません。

I don't eat much.

---

## ほとんど

**hotondo**

Almost / Mostly

Example:

ほとんど分かりません。

I hardly understand.

---

## 全然

**zenzen**

Not at all (with negative)

Example:

全然分かりません。

I don't understand at all.

---

# 2. Time Adverbs

## 今

**ima**

Now

Example:

今行きます。

I'm going now.

---

## もう

**mou**

Already / Soon

Examples:

もう食べました。

I already ate.

もうすぐ着きます。

I will arrive soon.

---

## まだ

**mada**

Still / Not yet

Examples:

まだです。

Not yet.

まだ勉強しています。

I'm still studying.

---

## すぐ

**sugu**

Immediately / Soon

Example:

すぐ行きます。

I'll go right away.

---

## 後で

**ato de**

Later

Example:

後で電話します。

I'll call later.

---

## 先に

**saki ni**

First / Before others

Example:

先に行きます。

I'll go first.

---

## 最近

**saikin**

Recently

Example:

最近忙しいです。

I've been busy recently.

---

## 昔

**mukashi**

Long ago / In the past

Example:

昔ここに住んでいました。

I used to live here.

**kanari**

# 8. Conversation Adverbs

## でも

**demo**

But / However

Example:

でも大丈夫です。

But it's okay.

---

## だから

**dakara**

Therefore / So

Example:

疲れた。だから寝ます。

I'm tired. So I will sleep.

---

## もちろん

**mochiron**

Of course

Example:

もちろん行きます。

Of course I'll go.

---

## 例えば

**tatoeba**

---

## 特に

**tokuni**

Especially

Example:

特に日本料理が好きです。

I especially like Japanese food.

---

## 実は

**jitsu wa**

Actually

Example:

---

# Rule For Speaking

A very natural Japanese sentence often follows:
[Time]
+
[Adverb]
+
[Topic]
+
[Object]
+
[Verb]


Example:

明日
たぶん
友達と
映画を
見ます。

Tomorrow, I will probably watch a movie with my friend.
