# Japanese Verbs.md

## How Japanese Verbs Work

Japanese verbs have three main forms:

| Form | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary Form | 食べる | to eat |
| ます Form | 食べます | eat / will eat (polite) |
| て Form | 食べて | eat and... / please eat / ongoing |

Example:

食べる  
→ 食べます  
→ 食べた  
→ 食べている  
→ 食べたい  
→ 食べられる  

One verb can create many sentences.
